20–22 Nov: gold tokens and the oracle disagreed all weekend

Gold markets shut at 22:00 UTC on Friday. While they were shut, the Chainlink XAU/USD feed on Arbitrum didn't settle on one price. In three bursts (Friday night, Saturday late morning, and Sunday afternoon into evening) it flipped back and forth between exactly two values, $3,538.18 and $3,575.31, about 1% apart, more than 50 times, and in between it went quiet for long stretches, including 24 hours from Saturday to Sunday. Tokenised gold kept trading between $3,500 and $3,553, up to −2.12% below the oracle when the oracle sat on its higher value (Sat 21 Nov 04:30 UTC). When gold reopened at 23:00 UTC on Sunday it traded around $3,581, and the tokens went back to their usual small discount (about 0.27%). Over the weekend the tokens drifted low, and even the oracle couldn't settle on a price.

That is the point: while the market is shut, nobody knows which price is right, and a lending protocol is relying on one of them.
